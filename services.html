Services

No matter what stage you're in,
support is available.

Breastfeeding changes as your baby grows, so support is organized the same way: by where you and your baby actually are, not a single checklist for every visit.

Prenatal

The perfect time to prepare for breastfeeding, before the questions are urgent.

  • Evidence-based strategies for common breastfeeding challenges
  • Assessment of maternal breast anatomy
  • Assessment of maternal health history
  • Creation of a breastfeeding support plan built before baby arrives

Newborns

The early days of infancy bring challenges few people see coming.

  • Achieving an effective, pain-free latch
  • Positioning
  • Weight gain concerns
  • Nipple pain
  • Engorgement
  • Mastitis
  • Low milk supply
  • Oversupply
  • Tongue-tie and other oral restrictions
  • Pumping
  • Flange sizing
  • Bottle feeding techniques

Older Babies

Breastfeeding can change so much as your baby grows.

  • Returning to work
  • Teething
  • Biting
  • Bottle refusal
  • Distracted eating
  • Nursing strikes
  • Return of menses or pregnancy
  • Weaning, on your timeline

How sessions work

In-home, in-office, or virtual

In-home visits across the Greater Sacramento area, in-office at the CuraNatal office in Orangevale, or virtual, whichever fits your family. Carrie is a provider through CuraNatal Health, and insurance or cash pay options are available at booking.

Beyond the basics

Oral restrictions and tongue-tie

Advanced training through the Oral Habilitation of the Breastfeeding Dyad masterclass means Carrie both assesses and supports tongue-tie and other oral restrictions many consultants aren't trained to catch, with referrals for collaborative care when that's the right next step.
